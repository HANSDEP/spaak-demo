Delivery & returns

Delivered ready to ride. Returned without fuss.

Our own courier brings your bike to the door, and you have 30 days to change your mind.

BikesOur own bike courier brings your bike assembled and adjusted, free in the Netherlands and Belgium, within 3 to 7 working days. Two working days before, you get a 4-hour window by email.
AccessoriesBy PostNL, free from €50, otherwise €4.95. Ordered on a working day before 17:00, usually delivered the next working day.
Returns30 days, no reason needed. Accessories unused and in their packaging, bikes undamaged and ridden less than 50 km.
RefundsWithin 5 working days after we have received and checked your return, to your original payment method.

Send back an accessory

  1. Fill in your order number and email address.
  2. You get a free PostNL return label by email.
  3. Drop the parcel off at any PostNL point.
This is a demo shop, so no label is sent.

Send back or exchange a bike

Bring it to our store for free, or our courier collects it for €49. Another frame size or colour is a free swap in one visit.
