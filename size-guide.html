Size guide

Which frame size fits you?

All spaak. bikes use the same chart. The frame size is the length of the seat tube, and your height is all you need to choose it.

The red tube is the frame size.

SizeFrameYour height
XS45 cm155 to 162 cm
S49 cm163 to 170 cm
M53 cm171 to 178 cm
L56 cm179 to 186 cm
XL60 cm187 to 197 cm

The Stad E comes in S to XL, the Stad and the Grind in XS to XL.

Between two sizes?The smaller size rides more upright and relaxed, the larger one more stretched and sporty.
Free fittingIn our store we measure you and set the saddle and handlebar. No appointment needed.
Wrong size after all?Exchange it within 30 days for free. Our courier swaps it in one visit.
Under 155 or over 197 cm?Come by for a fitting and we'll tell you honestly what fits.
